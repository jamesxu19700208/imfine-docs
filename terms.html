The App does not provide emergency, rescue, medical-diagnosis or health-monitoring services. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number immediately. The App cannot confirm whether you are actually safe, and an alert may be delayed or fail to send because of device settings, network conditions, carrier restrictions, battery optimization, or a third-party outage.
