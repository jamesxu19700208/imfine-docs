ImFine — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026  |  App: ImFine (com.imfine.app)

English简体中文

English

Summary in one sentence: ImFine works entirely on your device. Your emergency contacts, check-in records and settings never reach the developer — they are stored encrypted on your phone and are sent only by you, through your own channels (your email account, your email API key, your SMS, or your own group-bot webhooks).

1. Who we are

ImFine ("the App") is developed and operated as an independent software product. For privacy questions, contact: jamesxu1026@gmail.com.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not operate any server that stores your data.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data, contacts, check-in history or location.
  • We have no advertising SDKs and no analytics/tracking SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device only, and is encrypted at rest:

  • Emergency contacts (names, masked emails/phones you enter) — stored in an AES-256 encrypted database (SQLCipher).
  • Settings and message templates — stored in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • Check-in records and alert history — stored locally.
  • Optional API keys (e.g., a Resend.com email API key, or push keys you choose to save) — stored encrypted on-device and used only to send alerts to your chosen recipients.

You can delete all of it at any time via Settings → Privacy & disclaimer → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Data sent by you, through your own accounts

When an alert is triggered, the App composes and sends a message using channels you configured. The developer never sees this traffic:

  • Email via your device mail app: your device's standard email flow, governed by your email provider's privacy policy.
  • Email via Resend (optional): the message is sent from your own Resend account using your own API key, to recipients you entered. Resend processes this transmission under Resend's privacy policy.
  • SMS (optional, Pro): sent by your device and carrier as if you sent it yourself.
  • Group-bot webhooks (optional, Pro): messages go directly to the webhook URLs you configured (e.g., your own bot integration).

The recipients of alerts are people you explicitly added as emergency contacts. Make sure they expect to receive such messages.

5. Permissions

  • Internet — required to send alert emails/webhooks through your configured channels.
  • Notifications — check-in reminders and alert confirmations.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarm) — so check-in reminders fire at the configured time.
  • SMS (optional) — only if you enable SMS delivery.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Payments. The developer does not receive or store your payment details. Google handles your purchase data under the Google Privacy Policy. The App verifies your purchase status with Google Play solely to unlock Pro features (and to lock them again if a purchase is refunded or cancelled).

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: jamesxu1026@gmail.com.

简体中文

一句话总结：ImFine 完全在你的手机上运行。你的紧急联系人、签到记录与设置永远不会到达开发者——它们加密存储在你的设备上，并且只由你通过你自己的渠道（你的邮箱、你的邮件 API 密钥、你的短信、你自己的群机器人 Webhook）发出。

1. 我们是谁

ImFine（"本应用"）为独立开发的软件产品。隐私相关问题请联系：jamesxu1026@gmail.com。

2. 我们不收集的数据

  • 我们不运营任何存储你数据的服务器。
  • 我们不收集、传输、出售或共享你的个人数据、联系人、签到记录或位置。
  • 本应用不含广告 SDK，也不含统计/追踪 SDK。

3. 存储在你设备上的数据

以下数据仅保存在你的设备上，并已加密：

  • 紧急联系人（你填写的姓名、邮箱/电话）——存储于 AES-256 加密数据库（SQLCipher）。
  • 设置与消息模板——存储于 Android Keystore 加密的本地存储（EncryptedSharedPreferences）。
  • 签到记录与提醒历史——本地存储。
  • 可选的 API 密钥（如 Resend 邮件 API 密钥、推送密钥）——加密保存在设备上，仅用于向你指定的收件人发送提醒。

你可以随时通过「设置 → 隐私与免责声明 → 删除我的全部数据」删除全部数据，或直接卸载应用。

4. 由你通过自己的账户发送的数据

触发提醒时，本应用使用你配置的渠道发送消息，开发者无法看到这些流量：

  • 通过设备邮件应用发送：使用系统标准邮件流程，受你的邮件服务商隐私政策约束。
  • 通过 Resend 发送（可选）：使用你自己的 Resend 账户与 API 密钥发送到你指定的收件人，传输过程受 Resend 隐私政策约束。
  • 短信（可选，专业版）：由你的设备与运营商发出，等同于你本人发送。
  • 群机器人 Webhook（可选，专业版）：消息直接发送到你配置的 Webhook 地址。

提醒的接收者是你主动添加的紧急联系人，请确保对方知情并愿意接收此类消息。

5. 权限说明

  • 网络——通过你配置的渠道发送提醒邮件/Webhook。
  • 通知——签到提醒与警报确认。
  • 精确闹钟——保证签到提醒在设定时间触发。
  • 短信（可选）——仅在你启用短信发送时使用。

6. 内购

订阅与一次性购买完全由 Google Play 支付处理，开发者不会收到或存储你的支付信息，相关数据由 Google 按 Google 隐私政策处理。本应用仅向 Google Play 查询购买状态，用于解锁专业版功能（以及退款/取消订阅后重新锁定）。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人（包括儿童）的数据。

8. 政策变更

如本政策发生变化，新版本将在本页面发布并更新生效日期。

9. 联系方式

如有问题或请求：jamesxu1026@gmail.com。
